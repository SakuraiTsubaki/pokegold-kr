# Research Guide

Record the exact Korean target whenever differences may matter: release, revision, bank, section, address, text/font table, symbol, asset index, or other stable locator.

Confidence levels: **Hypothesis**, **Observed**, **Reproduced**, and **Matched**.

Preserve commands, tools, hashes, offsets, symbols, source references, and other evidence needed to reproduce important findings. Use `unknown` or `TBD` instead of invented metadata.
